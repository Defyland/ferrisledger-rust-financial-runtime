## Analysis

The load run stayed well under the configured thresholds of p95 < 750 ms,
p99 < 1200 ms, and error rate < 1%. The observed bottleneck is still JSONL
append and fsync under a single local file. The next optimization remains a
segmented file or PostgreSQL event-store adapter with a persisted idempotency
index.
